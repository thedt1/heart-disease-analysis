# Heart Disease Risk Factors

Analysis of 918 patient records to find which factors are most associated with heart disease.

**Live dashboard:** https://public.tableau.com/views/HeartDiseaseRiskFactors_17923659244460/HeartDiseaseRiskFactors 

! [Dashboard] (dashboard.png). 

## Dataset
Heart Failure Prediction dataset (Kaggle), 918 patients, about 55% with heart disease.

## Tools
SQL (SQLite), Tableau Public

## Key Findings
- Patients with exercise-induced angina had an 85.2% disease rate vs. 35.1% without
- Disease rate rises with age: 32.5% (under 40) to 73.1% (60+)
- Asymptomatic chest pain had the highest rate (79%), higher than typical angina (43.5%)
- Patients with heart disease had a lower max heart rate in every age group
